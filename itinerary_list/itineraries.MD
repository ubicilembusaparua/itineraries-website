# Morning weekend itineraries in Jakarta

Five day plans for two friends, built from the places in [places.csv](places.csv). Times are suggested, with room to linger over food or conversation. Allow extra travel time when traffic is heavy.

## 1. Chinatown breakfast and old Jakarta

**Best for:** street food, history, and photography · **Start:** 08:00, Saturday or Sunday · **Finish:** around 13:00

| Time | Stop | Do together |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 08:00–09:15 | Glodok and Petak Sembilan | Browse the streets, choose breakfast, and share a few snacks. |
| 09:15–10:00 | Candra Naya | Look at the historic house and its architectural details. |
| 10:00–10:30 | Transfer to Kota Tua | Take a short ride or walk if you want a longer outing. |
| 10:30–12:00 | Kota Tua and Kali Besar | Follow the canal, photograph the old buildings, and explore Fatahillah Square. |
| 12:00–13:00 | Lunch near Kota Tua | Pick a place together and end the outing at an easy pace. |

## 2. Lakeside walk and books in Cikini

**Best for:** a quieter catch-up · **Start:** 08:00, Saturday or Sunday · **Finish:** around 13:00

| Time | Stop | Do together |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 08:00–09:00 | Taman Situ Lembang | Walk around the lake and find a spot to sit and talk. |
| 09:00–09:45 | Breakfast and transfer to Cikini | Eat nearby, then walk or take a short ride to Taman Ismail Marzuki. |
| 09:45–11:30 | Perpustakaan Jakarta at Taman Ismail Marzuki | Each choose a book for the other to browse, then compare your picks. |
| 11:30–13:00 | Taman Ismail Marzuki and lunch in Cikini | Explore the public spaces or any exhibition open that day, then have lunch. |

The [Perpustakaan Jakarta listing](https://perpustakaan.jakarta.go.id/supports/about-us) gives weekend hours of 09:00–22:00.

## 3. Green morning, then Blok M

**Best for:** a walk followed by casual food and browsing · **Start:** 07:00, Saturday or Sunday · **Finish:** around 13:00

| Time | Stop | Do together |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 07:00–08:30 | Tebet Eco Park | Walk the paths and cross the elevated bridge while the day is still cool. |
| 08:30–09:30 | Breakfast in Tebet | Pause for a meal before moving across town. |
| 09:30–10:30 | Transfer to Blok M | Allow time for traffic; this is the longest move in the plan. |
| 10:30–11:30 | Taman Literasi Martha Christina Tiahahu | Browse the reading spaces and take a relaxed walk through the park. |
| 11:30–13:00 | Surrounding Blok M | Explore nearby shops and choose a lunch spot. |

[Tebet Eco Park](https://tebetecopark.jakarta.go.id/v144/t101/informasi) lists daily hours of 06:00–20:00 and free entry.

## 4. Park, museum, and lunch in Central Jakarta

**Best for:** history with an indoor break · **Start:** 07:30, Saturday or Sunday · **Finish:** around 13:30

| Time | Stop | Do together |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 07:30–08:15 | Taman Lapangan Banteng | Walk around the park and see the monument before the heat builds. |
| 08:15–09:00 | Transfer to Museum Nasional | Take a short ride and leave time to enter the museum. |
| 09:00–11:15 | Museum Nasional | Pick a section each and show your favorite object or story to your friend. |
| 11:15–12:00 | Transfer to Pos Bloc | Travel toward Pasar Baru. |
| 12:00–13:30 | Pos Bloc | Have lunch and browse the historic building and its creative spaces. |

[Museum Nasional](https://www.museumnasional.or.id/layanan-blu/) lists Friday–Sunday hours of 08:00–20:00; admission is paid.

## 5. City skyline and culture in Kuningan

**Best for:** a park walk and a small exhibition · **Start:** 07:00, **Saturday only** · **Finish:** around 12:30

| Time | Stop | Do together |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 07:00–08:30 | Hutan Kota GBK | Walk the grounds, enjoy the skyline, and sit for a while. |
| 08:30–10:00 | Breakfast and transfer to Kuningan | Eat before visiting Erasmus Huis, which opens at 10:00. |
| 10:00–11:30 | Erasmus Huis | Browse the library and the exhibition hall; choose one piece to discuss afterward. |
| 11:30–12:30 | Lunch in Kuningan | Finish with an unhurried meal. |

[Hutan Kota GBK](https://pusat.jakarta.go.id/kelurahan/kelurahan-gelora/fasilitas/fasilitas-taman-hutan-kota/taman-hutan-kota) lists a Saturday morning session of 06:00–10:00. [Erasmus Huis](https://www.netherlandsandyou.nl/web/indonesia/visit) opens Saturday 10:00–16:00, closes Sunday, and allows free visits to the library and exhibition hall without registration.
